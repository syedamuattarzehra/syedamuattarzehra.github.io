About

I like difficult subjects before they have been cleaned up for the reader.

Most of my work starts with material that is dense, technical or badly explained. I read through it, figure out what matters, then write it in a way that keeps the substance without making the reader work harder than necessary.

Finance became my main beat early in my writing career. Since then, I have worked across forex, broker products, trading technology, crypto and Web3, while handling everything from research and SEO briefs to final edits and publication.

I originally trained in Cardiac Perfusion at King Edward Medical University before moving into content. That background left me unusually picky about research, factual detail and the difference between sounding informed and actually being informed.
