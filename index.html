About

I like difficult subjects before they have been cleaned up for the reader.

Most of my work starts with material that is dense, technical or badly explained. I read through it, figure out what matters, then write it without sanding every sentence into corporate wallpaper.

Finance became my main beat early in my writing career. Since then, I have worked across forex, broker products, trading technology, crypto and Web3, handling research, SEO, website copy, social media, market commentary and publication.

I originally trained in Cardiac Perfusion at King Edward Medical University before moving into content. That background left me unusually picky about research, factual detail and the difference between sounding informed and actually being informed.

Social media copywriting

A chart may be complicated. The post beside it cannot be.

I write social copy for finance audiences who already have plenty competing for their attention. At Amber Markets, that includes market stories, trading education, platform updates, product-led posts, carousels and short copy written around the visual rather than dumped on top of it.

My older social work also includes single-post concepts on risk, trading psychology, breakouts, candlesticks, technical indicators and market basics. The format changed from post to post because the idea should decide the copy, not a template.
