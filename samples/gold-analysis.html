Is Gold's Bearish Cycle Over?

A market-analysis sample written when gold was trading around the $1,700s.

Gold entered the month with strong gains as the dollar weakened and U.S. jobs and inflation data surprised markets. The metal moved above $1,750 after rebounding from the $1,616 zone.

The piece looked at the pressure gold had faced during a year of aggressive rate hikes, stronger Treasury yields and a stronger U.S. dollar. It then examined the sharp November recovery and the possibility that a slower pace of Federal Reserve tightening could support bullion.

A small time-capsule moment

I wrote this when the $1,700s already felt like a serious level for gold. Looking back from a market where prices have since moved far beyond that range makes the piece a useful snapshot of how quickly “expensive” can age.
