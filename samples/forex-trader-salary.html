Forex Trader Salary: Could I Become Rich in 2022 and How?

A search-led educational piece on what determines a retail trader's returns and why the idea of a fixed “salary” does not fit forex trading.

The article explains that individual forex traders do not receive a traditional salary. Their results depend on account size, trading style, market conditions, leverage and the consistency of their approach.

What shapes trading returns

It covers starting capital, leverage and trading technique, with examples showing why identical percentage returns produce very different dollar outcomes on different account sizes.

The second half shifts into practical trading habits, including choosing a style that suits the trader, starting with manageable capital, keeping a written plan, reviewing past trades and treating risk management as part of the strategy rather than an afterthought.
